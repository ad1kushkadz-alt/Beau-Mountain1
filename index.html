A mountain is a part of the earth's crust that is higher than the area around it. Mountains usually have steep sides that significantly expose the bedrock. They are formed through tectonic forces or volcanism. These forces can locally raise the surface of the earth. Mountains erode slowly through the action of rivers, weather conditions, and glaciers. A few mountains are isolated summits, but most occur in huge mountain ranges.
